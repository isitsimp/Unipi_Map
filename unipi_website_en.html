Contact and Accessibility Information

The main Building of University of Piraeus is located at Karaolis & Dimitriou 80, 18534, Piraeus.

Access to the university is easy from every corner of Athens thanks to its location, as it is situated near two metro stations, a suburban railway station, a tram stop, and multiple bus stops.

Metro stations near the university:
• Piraeus (Line 1 and 3)
• Dimotiko Theatro (Line 3)

Train station near the university:
• Piraeus ( Train Departures | Live Suburban Railway Schedule Updates )

Tram stop near the University:
• Plateia Deligianni
• Evaggelistria

Bus stops near the university:
• St. Metro Piraeus: 909, 915, 20
• Venizelou: 040, 049, 130, 217, 218, 229, 500, 915, A1, B1, E90, X96
